## Position and execution

then make white/pink/gold easy to compare. Use instructions and formula support
this choice; they are not the opening purchase reason.

Sequence: product introduction → real skin film → three original shade photos
paired with original texture cutouts → moving pearl macro → four-person actual
use and concise application instructions → vegan formula/ingredients → product
closing → accessible product information.

Use Pretendard for Korean and Manrope for labels, with the same 36–72px hero
and 29–52px section-title ranges as Milky Cheek. Palette: #1a1a1a, #6f6a6b,
#925368, #f2e8e9, #f5f5f5, white. Section margins differentiate headings,
evidence and secondary copy. Mobile text does not simply shrink with a fixed
860px artwork canvas.

piece of the existing product information in a keyboard-operable disclosure.
